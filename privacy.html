Privacy Policy

FullYear Calendar  ·  Last updated: April 4, 2026

FullYear is a read-only calendar overview app for iOS. Your privacy is important. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is never collected.

What FullYear Does

FullYear reads your calendar events from Apple's EventKit framework and displays them in a yearly, monthly, or 3-month overview on your device. It does not create, edit, or delete events.

Data We Access

  • Calendar events — Read-only. Accessed via Apple EventKit with your explicit permission. Event data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any server.
  • Subscription status — If you purchase FullYear Pro, RevenueCat processes the transaction and stores an anonymous subscriber ID linked to your App Store account. No personal information (name, email, payment details) is shared with RevenueCat.
  • Widget data — A summary of your upcoming events is saved locally in an App Group shared between the app and its home screen widget. This data never leaves your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting services
  • No AI processing of your calendar data
  • No data stored on external servers by us

Third-Party Services

  • Apple EventKit — Calendar access framework provided by Apple. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management. Processes anonymous purchase receipts. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Calendar Permission

FullYear requests calendar access only to display your events. You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. Without calendar access the app shows an empty calendar.

Children's Privacy

FullYear does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app contains no account creation, social features, or data collection that would be of concern for younger users.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold. Since FullYear stores no personal data on our servers, there is nothing to request — all calendar data stays on your device under Apple's control.

For subscription-related data held by RevenueCat, contact them directly at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
